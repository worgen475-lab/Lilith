LILITH SERVICES — STATIC WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: landing page
- maps.html, events.html, endgame.html, quests.html, other.html: service order pages
- vouches.html: contact and vouch section
- style.css: dark fantasy violet / rose / gold theme
- app.js: selection, estimated totals, order summary generation, copy, and email draft

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the entire folder to a static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.).
3. Update the sample service options and prices in app.js under CATALOG.
4. Update your official contact link and genuine customer vouches in vouches.html.

Important:
- Prices and item names in app.js are starter examples because the supplied source only included the landing page markup and shared stylesheet, not the original per-page catalogs/pricing.
- “Send via email” opens the visitor's email app as a draft. To receive orders directly on a website, connect a form backend (for example Formspree, Google Forms, or your own server).
- No passwords, account credentials, or payment information are collected.
